The fork has diverged beyond a mirror. In addition to upstream visualization capabilities, it contains work on ontology-backed spatial intelligence, relationship derivation, scenarios, replay, operational briefs, provenance/confidence, GNSS-interference indications, deployment hardening, and additional data-source policy controls.

Third-party datasets, runtime providers, and bundled models are not automatically covered by the MIT source-code licence. Review `LICENSE`, `DATA_SOURCES.md`, model-specific notices, and the repository's automated data-licence policy before commercial distribution or deployment.

When syncing from upstream, preserve this provenance file and the original MIT attribution. Fork-specific issues and pull requests should target `Doomscro11/gods-eye-view`; upstream issues should only be filed when the behavior is reproducible against the upstream project.
